Qolom Olfactive Journal

Derived from the word "قلم" - "qolom" is pen in Bangla - in Islam it connotes the totality of ones life. The complete past, the current present, and all the potential futures - as we live through those times, we experience a variety of events.

Qolom shares these events through a meticulous blend of rare and ethereal natural materials. Evolving over time and ultimately becoming tangible experiences in ones own life.
