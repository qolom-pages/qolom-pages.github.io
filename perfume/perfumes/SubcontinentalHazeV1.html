Subcontinental Haze

Hazy Sun Drenched Marigold

This is the smell of the first step you take into the arid countries of the subcontinent - Pakistan, India, Bangladesh.

Focused on the IFRA-restricted material - marigold is the center, a luscious and deeply intoxicating flower - unashamedly so.

Primarily relying on the bountiful facets of Indian marigold, novel uses of mango leafs and cardamom help you experience the intoxicating air of the subcontinent.

With an almost harsh plume of an animalic nuanced mango, the journey starts of dirty and earthy with hints of mangoes left under the scorching heat.

The marigold comes in contact with your olfactory senses throughout, first with a green cannabis note, then to a deeply aromatic floral note.

At the drawdown, with more facets of the marigold unveiling itself, you start to smell an almost joyous festival of melted dark chocolate that slowly wakes up the clean barnyard Bengali oud.

Constrained to only 13 ingredient, only 10 being unique, the marigold has absolutely no chance to be overshadowed.

This is written on a test on two drops totalling ~0.5mm; at this range it breathes and condenses the experience of the arid dry South Asian Sun on your skin.
