Osmanthus Rock Ballad

Mid Tempo Sway

I was born in 1999, I appreciate music from 1970s, a very specific genre I enjoy is a "rock ballad"

For me, this genre of music always felt very well composed with great story-telling lyrics that felt as if you were listening to something that was read and alive with soul.

Osmanthus Rock Ballad is a symbolic gesture to rock ballads, centred around the cohesion of resinous leathery labdanum and honeyed sweet osmanthus.

With anything that is greatly moving, there's usually a sort of subtext in the narrative, secondary plots, or well constructed themes weaved into the story.

This was how I formulated this composition, I focused on building two seperate blends, one focused on labdanum and the other on osmanthus - the underlying ingredients were used to fortify the main characters.

The first half of this composition is an animalic earthy incense using honey notes from tobacco to marry the second half of the composition.

The second part is bright honey floral melange using champaca to meet the first part of the composition leveraging it's gothic incense notes.

The anchor for Osmanthus Rock Ballad is a Kynam CO2 that requires ample heat to be noticed, it allows the labdanum-osmanthus blend ebb and flow slowly over time.
