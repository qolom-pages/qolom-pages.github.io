Rainbow Rose

Three Coloured Rose

Red, white, and yellow - Turkish, Moldovan, and Chinese - three roses used in harmony to provide a full rose experience.

Rainbow Rose is a no fuss rose perfume that uses a roses in both an alchemic and olfactive purpose, using a perfume methodology developed over time.

Focused on the heart, a thick viscous Chinese yellow rose was used to provide a long lasting body, red rose to add a velveted feel, and white rose to act as the fleeting aura of the composition.

In my experience, the Chinese Pingyin rose is a sophisticated new-gen rose that provides subtlety due to the coastal terroir of the rose and the petal specific distillation, large amounts can be used without shifting balances.

An extremely precious red rose, an absolute from Turkey, solidifies at room temperature and permeates the perfume with obscuring visibility - a jammy velvet texture from the literal oils sprayed onto your skin.

Perfuming like tea, white rose from Moldova is peculiarly gentle, soft, and transparently airy that adds a top note to the heart of the perfume, it starts the transition into the beautiful rose duet of red and rose.

The opening and dry down of this composition play to specific facets of these roses - a fruity spiced elucidating the rose heart and a warm lactonic end that softly waves goodbye.

This was a V0.5 build meaning a test, no longer available for public use, but full of hope to be redone with much more deliberation in the future.
