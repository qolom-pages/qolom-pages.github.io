Magnolia Spritz

Revitalising Cleanse

Almost 40% oud, a composition so heavily weighted by dense, woody, feral animalic oils are completely transformed by fresh cut leaves and herbs.

A distillation of the original Grandeur blend, Magnolia Spritz formally began as a dense and heavy "thing" is now beautifully green and near-mentholic.

The homogamy of warm and green notes shows the epitome of what green tea in an olfaction lens can do, a great bridge between bright volatiles and slow releasing hearts.

Magnolia Spritz opens sharp and herby with unripe green mandarin that's just about to peak into juicy fruit tones.

Similar to modern day fougère, a bountiful blend of clary sage, timut pepper, and magnolia flower gives a sunkissed spring garden: warm but green.

Animalic Egyptian jasmine blended with Haitian and North Indian vetiver continues the trend of a clean farm vibe where the foliage has just peaked.

What I think is a nuanced use of a diluted vanilla helps bring the tone down to mellow out into the oud matrix that's supported by an oakmoss absolute.

The "Spritz" in Magnolia Spritz is the insignia of this composition is, slightly sparkling, refreshing, easy to wear oud majority blend.

Perfume Architecture

Top Green Mandarin
Top-Heart Magnolia Leaf and Flower, Neroli, Frankincense, French Clary Sage, Timut Pepper, Green Tea
Heart Egyptian Jasmine
Base-Heart Vetiver North India x Haiti, Vanilla
Base Oud Trat x Aged Cambodian, Oakmoss
