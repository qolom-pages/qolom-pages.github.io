Boozy Rong Chaa

Black Tea Wine

This is "Rong Chaa" - ironically meaning "coloured tea" in Bangla - there's no colour, it is 100% just tea and water.

The most consumed form of tea in India and Bangladesh is milky sugary tea that's the usual light brown cream colour, in Bangla that's "chaa" and "chai" is the spiced version.

"Rong chaa" is my choice of beverage when having tea, the crystal clear enigmatic amber red liquer of the tea leaves - that's the "colour" in "coloured tea"

The inspiration to build something around the experience of having a rong chaa came from a perfume called Dukkan by Elkhaldi Studio, a tea themed perfume with florals.

It very much reminded me of tea, however, there was something missing from it in relations to my memory - the scent of methi: fenugreek leaves.

I wanted a tea perfume that's is intoxicating but not a removal of what high quality tea can be; fruity and naturally sweet with a soul of its own.

You'll see in this composition, white grapes and blackcurrant, saffron and fenugreek, teas and florals; it acts like a memory but behaves like an unknown journey.

Boozy Rong Chaa is a winey take on traditional black tea fortifying it's own self with luscious florals and infused crushed Jin Jun Mei tea.
