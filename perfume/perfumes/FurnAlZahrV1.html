Furn Al Zahr

A Bakery Of Flowers

My life has three individuals, a French chocolatier, an indecisive Syrian, and a black cat named Lula - what do they have in common? Absolutely nothing.

As a joking commentary, I was asked to make a perfume for Lula who lives in the chocolate shop, thinking back I might have not noticed it was a joke and went ahead with the composition.

However I didn't want to focus on a single aspect; I wanted to represent core aspects of three people who have been there for me, three souls whose love I never had to buy.

Furn Al Zahr is in the Fusha dialect, it meaning "bakery of flowers" and it's an homage to my chocolate loving friend, the name itself has layers for those who look for them.

This composition tries to capture a few aspects of these three people (yes, Syrians are now people...) - buttery chocolate, the indolic national flower of Syria, and the very regal frankincense.

The blend presents itself as a gourmand but it's not classed as one, it more of a transformed floral, a soft velvety jasmine stuffed in a pain au chocolat that's been baked in an earthenware oven.

It starts off with a green edge from the galbanum, it hums down into a chocolate cookie with added instant coffee, all the while exhaling the jasmine and grounding Hajori that's been scaffolded by nagarmotha.

Furn Al Zahr isn't a composition that makes sense, it doesn't have clarity in it's translation between ingredients and olfactive experience, however it is unimaginably beautiful.
