Civil Dawn

The Layover Between Dawn And Dusk

Do you know what the best part of the day is? When the night is officially gone, the people are asleep, and the sun has just broken past the clouds.

That particular part of the Sunrise is called the "Civil Dawn" the exact boundary when the Sun starts to rise. The exact moment the day starts.

For me, this is the absolute best time to have a coffee, especially when it's drank after an all nighter and you're just ready to sleep.

I wanted to build something that represented this equinox of solace and civility, that's why I focused this blend on lavender, coffee, and benzoin

The lavender opening symbolises the calm empty mornings void of all the noises of a metropolitan city.

The coffee bridges the quiet with warm notes of spices and slight coconut-esque lactones to symbolise the last hurrah of silence before the world wakes up.

Drawing down, finally, is a somewhat spice benzoin with cinnamon and vanilla undertones merged with honeyed tobacco and creamy sandalwood.

With the core identity of Civil Dawn set, additional materials help amplify these aspects with the aide of cassie, vetiver, and tonka.
