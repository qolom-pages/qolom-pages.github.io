Overt Orris

New Age Orris

You have noir, fougère, and "purple" styles - Overt Orris is what I call "new age" - removing the vintage aspects of a chypre and replacing it with cooling and brighter materials.

Unlike most lipsticky or powdery perfumes that feature and spotlight the roots of an iris flower (orris), this one is more similar to a chypre.

The difference here is instead of leaning into a green mossy darker nuances, I enforced the orris with frankincense and opopanax which offer a metal-soft tone.

Pairing with the cooling notes, to add to this new age style, there are some almost pollen scented bright florals that replaces the usual dark chypre aspects.

Opening of the perfume hints at the orris that's underlying in the composition, starting with a bright timut pepper that's diluted in a juicy sunny citrus oil.

A very minute use of Egyptian violet, Chinese jasmine, Madagascan geranium making up less than 5% of the composition adds texture without removing focus from the orris transition.

My favourite aspects of this new age style orris is the use of floral absolutes instead of essential oils, adding both structure and depth that can only be experienced throughout the day.

Overt Orris isn't a typical powdery floral perfume, it's cooling, refreshing, and bright composition that unravels slowly like a lone cloud drifting in clear skies.
