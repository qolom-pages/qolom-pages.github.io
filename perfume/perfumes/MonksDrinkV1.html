Monk's Drink

Decadent, Sweet, and Warm

Part of the previously named "grandeur" line - Monk's Drink is the separation of a prior blend that was too dense and complex to be utilised.

Boasting with 33% oud with a mixture of clean Trat oil supplemented with a more dark burnt fruity notes that is greatly welcomed into this composition.

Similar to a warm chai loaded with cardamom or the Arabic coffee "qahwa" that's often sweetened and spiced with saffron, the core of this blend is this welcoming sweet warm drink.

The sweet aspect is unconventional, no sugar or syrups are used, instead a creamy coconut CO2 and a jammy Turkish rose helps bring decadent notes.

Imagine bitter condensed milk flooded Vietnamese coffee and spicy-sweet chai - this is the absolute opening.

An incredibly welcoming and warm opening isn't all there is, dusty woods with moreish tobacco draws it down to it's core.

Deeply resinous labdanum and the meditative benzoin helps anchor the indulgence, reminding us that this is in fact an homage and not a consumeristic sweet treat.

Monk's Drink is this homage, a view into the past where the Sufi Saints drank coffee and ate dates in the deep nights performing prayers.
