Sultani Ghāliyah

An Exercise In Constraint

Sultani Ghāliyah, a ghāliyah for royalty, cannot be made without involving those around you - the name comes from the community when asked "what shall I name this?".

A ghāliyah has always about precious, expensive, rare materials; and whilst that may be a requirement there is so much more to the story and history.

To the most accurate description (albeit debate on methods and rations) of a ghāliyah is a mixture of rose, oud, ambergris, saffron, musk, and sandalwood - each providing a pillar to the composition.

Whilst the use of expensive and precious materials are important for something dubbed "ghāliyah" we often forget the most important, that is the use of purposeful design in constructing something truly precious.

The design for this ghāliyah is the use of dragons blood processed in-house but like I said it requires a purposeful hand - the muse here is dialled to only 1.25% because it has it's role and plays it well.

The usual suspects are present: an aged rose, wild oud, brown ambergris resin, saffron resin, and an aged multi-step macerated musk in sandalwood.

A ruby red composition that has such a quiet confidence, a true act of constraint, rare materials sewn together with the most precious and noble of elements; caution, care, and calculation - the human intellect given freedom.

Sensually intimate, as your blood pulses through your veins it blooms with a sense of solemn calm, honoured and precious.
