Laal Bangla 0.5

Butter Soft Champaca

A collusion between a gift and an ode to the smokey rural night-time life of Bangladesh when alight with incense and flowers.

Used primarily in pre-partition rituals, sacred incensing, and religious ceremonies; red champaca is a flower with a waxy resin smell infused with jasmine-like facets.

Whilst not used at all in Islamic settings, this flower is sacred to Hindus and Buddhists - many of whom are part of the multi-faith co-existence of Bangladesh.

Without attuning any religious sentiment to the composition, I feel like accesses the parts of my memory tied to that land that's so heavily storied in religion.

The deeply interwoven society of Bangladesh has an intense collusion of people, culture, and history that I believe this composition achieves without exceeding limits and entering areas of mockery, mimicry, or appropriation.

In comparatively tiny amounts, red champaca takes the stage of this perfume with magnolia which is almost 4x the ratio by weight plays a supporting role to lift and diffuse such a complex material.

Mandarin and bergamot open the gates of this olfactive exploration, a dilution of mango leaf absolute forms a bridge into a mix of warm benzoin, dense tobacco, and most importantly Bengali oud.

Completely diluted to a functional 27% using only a white rice tincture, this perfume is buttery soft with a sweet citrus opening and a deep champaca heart.

Perfume Architecture

Top Mandarin, Bergamot
Top-Heart Magnolia Flower, Ho Wood
Heart Red Champaca, Rose, Cinnamon
Base-Heart Mango Leaf, Tobacco
Base Light Bangla Oud, Sumatran Benzoin
