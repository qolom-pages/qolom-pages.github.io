The Gentleman 0.5

Everyday Pre-Modern Fougère

The Gentleman (version 0.5) was a blend made to mimic old French perfumery that focuses on typical men's style perfume.

Owing to it's basic composition, it's easy wearing; owing to its quality of the materials, it has beautiful performance with synergetic chemistry.

The use of intentional natural materials, instead of singular-molecules, means that throughout its wear and ageing it will profoundly change as the plethora of organic materials interact.

Classic pairings of clary sage, bergamot, and lavender give a masculine leaning start; almost all to common with pre-modern fougère.

A soft clean wood core sets this apart from simplicity using a green frankincense and green patchouli distillation to set you into a "heart of the forest" landscape.

Not atypical, the base of The Gentleman is cedarwood and tobacco, bound together with a Siam Benzoin that provides a warm cinnamic vanilla warmth.

The name, The Gentleman, is a nod to this style of perfume but not to the absolute nature of this composition - it's very much thematic to a citrus-wood-resin flow that works with your attire and not gender.

This was a V0.5 build meaning a test, no longer available for public use, but full of hope to be redone with much more deliberation in the future.
