Sweet Sunrise

Endless Strawberry Evolution

A blend of unintended consequences, what was meant to be the intersection between rose and oud and strawberry and rice ended up being a endless olfactory sensation.

The original inspiration came from a layered blend of Sabah Al Hind and Strawberry Mochi by Zakir Gafur and d'Annam respectively - would have called it "Ichigo at Ishraq" in another lifetime.

Ichigo meaning strawberry in Japanese and Ishraq meaning sunrise in Arabic, I had formulated something to be sweet with a rice-like warmth on top of the archetypical rose-oud blend.

What did happen however was a composition that broke the concept of typical rose-oud and ended up being strawberry, strawberry, strawberry, and more strawberry.

Keeping with the Japanese mochi theme, the transition between sweet jammy strawberry to a subtle rose and Hindi style oud blend was a mixture of rice and hay giving the sensation of freshly baked rice.

By contrast, this blend only has 10 ingredients, 36% by weight oud, and shockingly expense to make; the numbers and stats here have no reason being like this for such a deviously nuanced blend.

A quick descriptor of Sweet Sunrise: a preliminary flash of rose that opens up to a candied yuzu in a pot of strawberry compote, steam of freshly cooked rice layered onto wild Thai oud, sweet slow releasing Chinese Oud, and textured Hindi.

Sweet Sunrise does capture the essence of strawberries used in a magnitude of different ways; fresh, jammed, the leaves, the sugars - it is oud and rose and strawberry and rice.
