Bab Al Khayal

Deep Dark Purple

Bab Al Khayal is the door to imagination, several 10s of materials used to develop a composition that will objectively perform and bloom differently for everyone.

The original concept of this was to make something that was aromatically "purple" - a buzzword in perfumery to portray something mysterious and elusive, typical components are: patchoulis, old florals, wild ouds, fruit tincture, and animal musk.

In my mental image, a "purple" aromatic profile is something that's alluring yet hard to describe, a deep transmutation of materials that have been synergised exquisitely to becoming greater than the sum of their parts.

Obviously, one can just dump several hundred materials into a bottle and say they've made something amazing, something more that what was added - by the sheer chemical diversity it's impossible not to.

I've owned and still do own and have access to perhaps thousands of ingredients that I could dump into a bottle - 48 selected materials including some homemade were used to form this very specific blend.

While I personally believe that 48 is way too much for a natural perfume, you can cause clashes, redundancies, and even spoilage when one or more materials interact adversely - these 48 aren't used for just the aroma.

To add aroma I used fruits, to add texture I added musk, to add depths several ouds were used, to add a sense of "je ne sais quoi" I used aged and narcotic florals.

Bab Al Khayal is whatever you think it is, it reacts with your surrounding and skin chemistry, it truly is whatever your imagination thinks it is.
