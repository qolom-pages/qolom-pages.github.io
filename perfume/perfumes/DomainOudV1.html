Domain Oud

Domain Expansion: Meteoric Oud Trail

"Oh, perhaps I should make a simple daily wear easy going oud based perfume."

That did not happen in the slightest, whilst many easy wearing oud based perfumes exist in natural, synthetic, and mixed media compositions - I don't think oud should be muffled down into quietness.

At 30% concentration, this perfume in of it self is easy to wear, although it's easy to wear it is also a complex composition with character and identity.

Domain Oud is named after the well known manga/anime Jujutsu Kaisen where they have a power system called "domain expansion" that projects ones identity into the physical world.

I wanted to build a perfume based on this, the identity of oud given a sensory shape that integrates with your surrounding and daily life.

The start of the perfumes presentation is a fruit punch opening made with in-house extracted fruit materials that represents the slow releasing sweetness of oud.

The heart and transitory properties are built using a mixture of jasmine and white lotus that have blended almost seamlessly giving soft round edges to this composition.

Domain Oud finally projects itself to your senses using a mix of wild and cultivated oud primarily from Thailand that ultimately serves as it's physical conduit to the physical world.

Perfume Architecture

Top Egyptian Strawberry, Congnac, Bergamot
Top-Heart Blackcurrant
Heart White Lotus, Rose, Hainan Oud
Base-Heart Indian Jasmine Heart, Anhui Green Tea, Yunnan Pu'er 1996
Base Wild Central Thai Oud, Traditional Oud Bangla x Thai, Khalas Date Seeds
