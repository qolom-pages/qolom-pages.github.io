Ghāliyah CoMaceration

Ghāliyah CoMaceration, or as I call it in my head "Ghāliyah CoMac", is 1:1 replication of historically mentioned ghāliyah made of: oud, saffron, musk, sandalwood, rose, and ambergris.

This is the result of a conversation that contained the plead to be more logical in my information seeking and ideas - quest for information was to find out if a ghāliyah could be made as a material instead of complete product, such that it could be *added* to other compositions as an ingredient, logically it had to be made it.

Pairing with Oudique, I sourced some white ambergris that was graciously ground down and mixed with equal amounts of the aforementioned materials above (a complete 1:1), left sealed and unopened for more than 6 months.

I have no reference on how to describe this, it hasn't been opened, it has taken almost £1000 and more than half a year with constant back and forth and planning to make only 20g - that's all that exists in this world.
