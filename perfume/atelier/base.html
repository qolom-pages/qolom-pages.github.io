Rose Waltz is an idea: what if rose was dark, alluring, and seductive. Something the draws you in, something that isn't the typical bright floral.

The basic idea most perfumers go for is a "noir" style - with gothic notes and animalic facets.

However, this rose doesn't focus on removing the sweet jammy essence of rose, in fact, it colludes with it with chai-like spices and a moreish dark coffee.

Opening with a Yemeni coffee tincture that's at the base of the dilutant, it comforts you with gentle intoxication.
